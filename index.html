About Us

The phonetics group at Penn emphasizes the interdisciplinary and experimental nature of phonetics in both teaching and research. The group is engaged in a wide range of research topics, including but not limited to laboratory studies of speech production and perception, prosody modelling, phonetic patterns and variation in large speech corpora, and integration of phonetics and speech technology.
